# M7 — acceptance, as it was earned

Five criteria were fixed when M7 began ([ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), M7). This is each one's verdict at close, and it is the verdict the evidence earned and not the one the wording
of the criterion reads as. A `Partial` says what part. Where a criterion rests on a mechanism and not on a measurement, the mechanism is credited as a mechanism.

| # | Criterion | Verdict | What the verdict rests on | What was not, and what would unblock it |
|---|---|---|---|---|

The state a learner is shown is rebuilt from the stored responses by the same function and equals the stored row, and the evidence ids it names are exactly the rows behind it. **Two pass and three are partial; none is blocked outright.** The two that pass are mechanisms of the learning loop and are shown against a real database. The three that are partial
each have a part that is true and a part that is not: the report is fast and has not been put under load; the tap is one and leads nowhere; the events conform and are few.

## What this table did not find

A table of five passes would have been unchecked. Two things were found by checking it. The roadmap marked row 4 `met as written` and the register held D87 open against it in the
same breath: the wording was met and the purpose was not, and the table says which. And the ROADMAP's own status notes for Tracks E to H2 were dated up to five days after the
commits they describe, on an assumed one-track-a-day cadence; they now carry the dates of those commits.
